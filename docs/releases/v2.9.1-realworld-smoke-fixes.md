# v2.9.1 — Real-world Smoke Test Fixes

## Why
After v2.9 was tagged, five unseen real-world smoke cases exposed three small but important fact-preservation gaps:

- `estimated_cost` was dropped from a billing pre-check condition.
- `$4,500` total budget was dropped from a meeting-note summary.
- `invoice_id` could be dropped from the first line of a JSON-heavy billing response.

These were not architecture failures; they were feature/scoring gaps in the fact-preserving path.

## Changes

- Added snake_case identifier detection, e.g. `estimated_cost`, `invoice_id`, `alert_threshold`.
- Added comparison/operator detection for conditions such as `balance < estimated_cost`.
- Increased scoring for invoice/customer/region/alert-threshold/total-budget terms.
- Adjusted short fact-dense handling: if content is short, fact-heavy, and the policy would only save a few tokens (`keep_ratio >= 0.90`), return no-op instead of risking the loss of a critical condition or derived total.
